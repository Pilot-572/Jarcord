Privacy

In force from 6 September 2026.

Jarcord is a Discord bot. It keeps the records a faction asks it to keep, inside a database that belongs to the person running the bot, and nothing else.

What is stored

  • Discord user ids and server ids, so a record can be tied to a member and a server.
  • Op signups, who was marked as attended or no-showed, and ratings with their notes.
  • Rank changes, warnings and the reason given for each.
  • Ticket form answers and the transcript of a ticket channel when it closes.
  • A message count and a last-seen time per member. Message text is not stored.
  • The Roblox username a member links, and the continent and unit they choose.
  • Server settings: which channels and roles the bot uses.

What is not done with it

Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared with anyone outside the server it came from. No message content is used to train a model.

The /ask command sends the question, the server's information panels, its settings and the asker's own record to the language model provider the bot is configured with, and nobody else's record. Ask the people running your server whether it is switched on.

Deleting it

Removing the bot from a server deletes that server's records within 30 days. A member can ask the people running their server for a copy of their record or for its deletion.
