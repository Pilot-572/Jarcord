Closing the op is how attendance gets recorded

The member picker opens with everyone marked Attending already ticked. The host unticks the no-shows and ticks the walk-ins. Anyone who said they were coming and was not picked is logged as a no-show.

The card closes with who came

The card loses its buttons and shows the turnout. Every name picked is filed as attended and every name missing as no-showed, on that member's record. The op thread gets the same line, then archives.

The promotion and the profile read from the same lines

/promote moves a member one step up the ladder and posts the notice with their record and the reason, so a promotion rests on attended ops rather than on a feeling.

/profile shows signed up, attended and no-showed for any member, with rank, unit, continent, warnings, rating, messages and last seen.

Set up in six commands

All six need Manage Server and are run once, in this order.

  1. /op-setup #ops @Members

    Where ops get posted and who gets pinged.

  2. /officer-role @Officers

    The role that may post ops, promote, warn and run tickets without holding Manage Server.

  3. /verify-setup #verify

    The channel new members are greeted in. The Verify button walks them through a short private flow.

  4. /ranks-setup

    Create every rank from Private 1 to Staff Sergeant and start every verified member without a rank on Private 1.

  5. /tickets-setup

    Create the ticket panel and the log channel, and post the panel.

  6. /logs-setup #command-log

    Write every notable action to this channel: promotions, warnings, tickets, ops posted and closed.

/op create

Post the first op card with RSVP buttons. Closing it later is what starts the record.
