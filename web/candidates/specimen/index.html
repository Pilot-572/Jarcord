The picker opens with everyone marked Attending already ticked. Untick the no-shows, tick the walk-ins. Whoever said they were coming and was not picked is logged as a no-show. The promotion notice and the profile read from the same record.

Set up in six commands.

Run them once, in this order. Each one needs Manage Server. Then post the first op.

  1. /op-setup #ops @Members

    Where ops get posted and who gets pinged when one goes up.

  2. /officer-role @Officers

    The role that posts ops, promotes, warns and handles tickets without being handed Manage Server.

  3. /verify-setup #verify

    The channel new members are greeted in. Verifying puts them on Private 1, the bottom of the ladder.

  4. /ranks-setup

    Creates every rank from Private 1 to Staff Sergeant, the NCO marker and the position roles that are missing.

  5. /tickets-setup

    Posts the ticket panel and creates the ticket channels you have not made yourself.

  6. /logs-setup #command-log

    Writes every notable action to this channel: promotions, warnings, tickets, ops posted and closed.

/op create
