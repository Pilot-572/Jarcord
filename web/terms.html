The free plan is provided as is. There is no promise of uptime, and the bot may be changed or withdrawn. Records are kept as described on the privacy page.

Jarcord+

A Jarcord+ pass is bought once and redeemed in one server with a code. It can be moved to another server at most once every 30 days. A pass consumed in a server is not refundable, except where the law where you live says otherwise. Prices and what each pass includes are listed on the Plus page and may change for new purchases.
